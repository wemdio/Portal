The workflow is evolving. Here's what the first engagement looks like today.

Is this a self-serve SaaS product?

Not yet. OutreachOS supports the research and campaign workflow, while Polza's team helps configure and review the first tests. We are automating more of the process over time.

Does AI write and send everything automatically?

AI helps research companies and prepare tailored drafts. A human reviews the approach, and the team currently helps configure and activate campaigns.

Do you guarantee a number of leads?

For selected niches, yes. We first confirm the audience, geography, lead definition, and reachable volume, then agree the monthly target before launch. We don't apply one generic guarantee to every market.

OutreachOS is built by the team at Polza Agency, drawing on four years of B2B client work in lead generation and outreach.
