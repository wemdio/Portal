Read-only production diagnosis, 2026-09-17, Portal VE2.

A project resumed four existing preview bases twice on the same day. All ended with Serper billing failure, but the project's provider journal recorded 37 successful Requesty attempts and no fresh Serper attempt that day. The shared Serper journal had 446 successful HTTP 200 attempts in the preceding hour. This does not establish the exact account balance.

Each saved relevance checkpoint retained 2–4 legacy website provider_error.kind=billing entries with no reader_revision/provider_error_attempts. Bases remained in search_policy.phase=existing. The deployed worker bundle confirms that the final relevance loop propagates saved website.provider_error.message and marks permanentFailure even when that entry was not retried in this pass. Fresh entries are prioritized ahead of errors under the per-pass website cap (32), so a legacy billing entry can keep terminating the pass and appear as a new lack of funds without a new provider rejection.

Saved reserve rows remain present; ready projection is empty. Do not describe reserves or constructor validation counts as ready contacts, or say bases were deleted.
