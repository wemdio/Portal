## Correction: per-reply verdict is not a newly created lead

User challenged audit case 9: an existing lead should not count as new when its conversation continues. Follow-up read-only checks on 2026-10-20 confirmed:

- Qualification 9a49c13b-b12a-471d-ab45-9f82d0b1c3da is a continuation of the prior winner, not the thread's specialist-alert claimant.
- One project board row contains the original and follow-up reply identifiers; one specialist notification was sent. No new handoff/forwarding for the follow-up.
- The OutreachOS CRM integration created a deal on the first reply and appended a note to that same open deal on this reply. No second CRM deal in this case.
- The separate OutreachOS General Telegram channel DID send a second message. Both original and follow-up use the heading “Новый лид OutreachOS”, even when the follow-up CRM action is note_added. Therefore the verified defect is misleading repeated new-lead notification in that separate channel, not duplicate board/deal creation.

Root cause in current code: notifyClientOfReply enqueues each affirmative qualification through enqueueOutreachOsReplyLead independently of specialistThreadClaim; the OutreachOS queue deduplicates reply events by qualificationId, not the thread. A new reply is a distinct event and produces another General notification. Do not suppress CRM conversation updates blindly when fixing the alert semantics.

The audit's raw qualification counts count replies; they are not unique-lead counts. Other dashboards were not audited in this check. No policy excluding B2C was approved by the user.
