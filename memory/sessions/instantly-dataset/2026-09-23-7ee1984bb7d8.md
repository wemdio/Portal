# WMD ticket notification, Mailganer project gap and guest-board audit

- Read-only operational snapshot 2026-09-23: three WMD.RU messages on one provider campaign/thread were complete automatic ticket-created notices. The Aug 31 notice had already been classified `not_lead`; Sep 16 and Sep 23 were `pending/ownership` because the notices were addressed to a mailbox other than the campaign sender. The early machine-reply filter did not recognize the complete WMD template, so ownership ran first and deferred.
- In `codex/wmd-ticket-guard` (based on `origin/Sergey` at `f78f31b67`), a sender- and subject-specific, whole-body WMD envelope check classifies only matching ticket notices as `service_acknowledgement`. A different sender, mismatched ticket number, or appended commercial request fails open. Local replay of the three sanitized real bodies: all three classified; all three with appended buyer request remained unclassified. Existing qualifier/worker tests (267) passed; core TypeScript and ESLint passed. No production writes or replay were performed.
- A Sep 23 `lead` without `qualified_project_id` came from the additional Instantly workspace `account-2`, campaign `Высокий скор`. Its outbound identified Mailganer; Portal has a Mailganer project, but this campaign has no project or period link. All 16 campaigns in `account-2` were unlinked at the snapshot; nine other Mailganer campaigns linked to the Portal project were in `main`. The campaign predated the Portal project. Sixty-four `lead` rows on this campaign had no project at the snapshot. This is a mapping gap, not proof that every `account-2` campaign belongs to Mailganer; no links were changed.
- The fixed set of 18 project-board rows seen on Sep 23 had 0 empty requests, 6 empty names, 7 empty organizations and 7 empty phones. In the new authored responses, six absent names had no trustworthy self-identification. Six of seven blank organizations appeared in the author’s own text/signature but missed the conservative parser (one had only a domain/old outbound name). Six of seven blank phones were absent from authored content; the remaining row had two numbers in a signature after a forwarded quote, which the current boundary logic missed. Rows kept unchanged; this audit does not authorize bulk backfill or quoted-contact guessing.
