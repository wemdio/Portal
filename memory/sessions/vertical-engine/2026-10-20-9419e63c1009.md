## Request and diagnosis

User reported that the active HST collection appeared stopped. Read-only production checks at 2026-10-20 21:21–21:25 UTC (2026-10-21 01:21–01:25 Asia/Tbilisi) showed base_collect running, no base error or budget hold, and company qualification advancing 98 → 106 → 121 of 281 companies. Confirmed spend at last snapshot was $0.041034, reserved $0; autopilot false and no template for this base. SMTP/website constructor had already completed. Its 656 valid_count meant OK + catch-all after dedup/contact limits, BEFORE final company qualification, not final delivered contacts.

The ENG screen nevertheless displayed “Some company checks stopped” and hid the progress counter. eng_company_qualification_progress.stop_reason=qualification_incomplete can arise from one unverified company while the remaining companies are still being checked. The React view treated any qualification note as a terminal state before reading status.
