## Request and behavior
User requested a visible “Скачать все проверенные контакты” action without the preview limit of 500, before launch and after daily replenishment, applying current project INN/domain exclusions. Implemented per hypothesis on Bases and Results. Each click downloads a fresh cumulative CSV of saved verified automatic bases, daily cohorts and owned delivery snapshots, deduplicated by normalized email. It does not fetch contacts manually added outside Portal to Instantly.

## Implementation and safeguards
New read-only GET `/api/tools/vertical-engine-v2/projects/[id]/contacts/export?hypothesis_id=...`, authenticated internal tool access and project/hypothesis scope. `verifiedContactsExport.ts` uses current pure launch quality gates and paginated metadata/history reads, without collect_info/raw candidates, RPC, job start, reconciliation or provider/model calls. No 500 output cap. Older shared auto bases with NULL hypothesis are read as facts only through exact owned launch items; unrelated shared rows are never dumped. Existing accepted/uncertain payloads remain downloadable, while explicit current quality rejects and lone skipped snapshots cannot resurrect contacts. Current exclusion matches on source identity or payload remove rows; changed policy revision or failed/incomplete read returns an error, never a partial CSV. No extra paid work, no migration. Missing identifiers retain the previously approved exact-match limitation.
