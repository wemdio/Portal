Implemented bounded recovery of historical Serper billing/configuration failures in VE2 relevanceGate.

- Prioritize persisted billing/key failures ahead of fresh website candidates on resume (saved text refinement retains first priority). The existing website cap and existing-first paid-search policy remain unchanged.
- Apply the exhausted-attempt guard only to transient failures; a previous balance/key failure must not permanently prevent recovery after an operator resumes.
- Only freshly observed provider failures can reintroduce a billing/configuration stop. Saved failures outside the current pass become needs_review, retain their error checkpoint, and are durably saved for later review. Evidence and completion markers are removed from those uncertain decisions; they remain excluded from delivery.
- Reset the provider-error attempt counter when its failure kind changes, so old billing failures do not consume the transient retry allowance.
