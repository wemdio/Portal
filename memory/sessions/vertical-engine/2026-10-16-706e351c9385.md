Implemented opt-in VE2 upload policy `daily | all_ready`, prompted by Alial's two prepared campaigns waiting behind the project's already-used daily allowance. The target (20,000) remains the common project goal, not a per-day or per-hypothesis allowance. No measurement of infrastructure utilization percentage is used.

`all_ready` uploads ready stock within remaining contract headroom, independent of Portal weekday/daily quota. Transport reservation is bounded to 5,000 rows; later sweeps continue in the same day. Existing ledgers, campaign ownership, exclusions, approval checks, uncertain-result fences and workspace-capacity pause remain. Never-started drafts can receive multiple batches without automatic activation; a campaign paused after first Start remains paused. Existing projects default to `daily`; no production settings were changed.

Sending settings use the already-approved campaign ceiling 1000 (including daily_max_leads in all_ready), tracking off; mailbox limits remain unchanged. Collection forecasts and per-batch limits are separate from uploading inventory.

Review caught and fixed three integration pitfalls: paused old reservations must be filtered from both replay and fresh RPC responses; changing upload mode before first launch must retain a valid sender forecast; moving ready inventory into Instantly must not trigger repeated paid collection. For all_ready, the two-workday supply buffer now counts uploaded-but-uncontacted inventory per campaign/item, and new collection is bounded by shared remaining headroom. Unknown analytics fail closed. Daily behavior remains unchanged.
