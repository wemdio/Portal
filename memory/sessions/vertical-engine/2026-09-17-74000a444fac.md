User asked for an actionable way to reduce current costs after the DeepInfra pilot did not establish equivalent quality. Implemented focused VE2 savings without changing working models, prompts, email validation, admission requirements, Serper policy, ENG or the other developer's cache.

`relevanceGate.ts`: (1) initial rows with no textual activity evidence in description/category/website text receive the same `needs_review` state without a paid classifier request. Existing `supportedDecision` could not admit/reject on names, URLs, vacancies or numeric codes alone. Filter before batching; checkpoint once and keep normal website/discovery follow-up and source caps. (2) semantic review remainders coalesce across classifier/website batches, still <=8 companies with the original schema/model/guards. Defer at most three classifier batches, flush before website selection and return, immediately flush recovered work; malformed retries remain single-company and bounded. Existing checkpoint context/hash is retained, avoiding global reclassification of paid results.

Validation: 14 existing suites / 109 assertions passed in ~4s, tsc --noEmit, ESLint modified files, diff --check. Existing regression block updated; no new it() or test files. Extra offline A/B script compared source at 1065c664d to changed code on identical synthetic inputs/provider replies: 24 sparse companies, 24 identical site checks, same 6 qualified companies, 8 -> 4 model requests. Another scenario preserved 9 qualified with 8 -> 5 requests and review batches [8,1]. Simulated interruption before pending review resumed with 5 total requests, 24 unique site visits, no reclassification/re-fetch; replay of completed results made zero requests. These are request-count savings, NOT a claim of 37.5–50% lower production invoice or measured real-data accuracy.
