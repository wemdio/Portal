- Read-only production audit on 2026-10-05 found two client replies accepted by Instantly via `/emails/test` for an out-of-campaign inbound address. Audit `to_email` matched the actual inbound sender, and personal `client_email_replies` markers existed, but `/emails/test` created no sent Unibox item; this made an accepted send look lost. Provider acceptance does not prove final delivery.
- For the affected client's two campaigns, 33 human qualifications were out-of-campaign (17 older than the 30-day stray feed window). Of 573 saved human qualifications with Instantly email IDs, 275 lacked `eaccount`, mostly older linked replies. The old email search relied on a bounded provider window/deep search and could silently return an empty list when one campaign's provider search was rate-limited.
- Change committed on `codex/client-reply-recipient` at `bf39329ed`: exact-address search consults the durable qualification index in both general and campaign replies; out-of-campaign reply sends first persist an outbox intent, then record provider acceptance and display the result in Portal's thread. Unknown outcomes remain visibly pending, and daily health reporting counts tracking failures. The Instantly DB migration is `20261005_0001_client_reply_outbox.sql`. Existing focused tests and strict TypeScript check passed. Production deployment was not performed in this task.
